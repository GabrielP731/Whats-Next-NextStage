The game's story centers around Orion, who, after interacting with a time machine that looks like a tower, travels to the medieval era—specifically, to the outskirts of a kingdom. This kingdom mistakes Orion for an enemy intent on destroying their castle. Consequently, your objective is to play as Orion and survive multiple waves of enemies while the time portal recharges, allowing you to escape back to your own time.

The game is an RPG/Survival Shooter hybrid in which you must eliminate all enemies attempting to breach your base. As you progress, your base becomes sturdier and more resilient; simultaneously, you gain power through a separate upgrade system.

Controls:
Mouse Movement: Aim/Adjust bullet trajectory
Left Click: Fire
E: Purchase an Upgrade Card (if funds are available)
Three Enemy Types will attack the player:
Tank: The Tank is the simplest of the enemy types, but has the highest attack and health, slowly walking towards the player.
Swordsman: The Swordsman is frail, but fast, so much so that it will dodge the first bullet that the player fires at it.
Ranger: The ranger is slower, but will patrol around the outer sides of the map, firing projectiles from a distance.
Upgrade System:
Defeated enemies drop coins. These coins are spent for the opportunity to draw from a random pool of cards that upgrade the tower’s damage, endurance, and attack speed. The cost increases each time that a card is drawn.
There is also an XP system where the tower levels up, gaining more health.
Cheat Keys:
C: Gain 10,000 coins
Ctrl: View stats and multipliers
L: Level up the tower

Objective: Survive 5 Waves of enemies.

Game Theme: Evolution. This theme is demonstrated at Tower Levels 3 and 5, where the tower transforms into a larger, more powerful structure. The card system also represents a form of progression; you begin with minimal damage output and defenses, but—provided you acquire enough upgrade cards—you can eventually become an indestructible force. Furthermore, the number of enemies increases with each wave, and their stats progressively rise for every wave the player successfully completes.

Regarding the "Graduation Project" requirements: The minimap fulfills the "View/Perspective" criteria. The enemies are implemented using inheritance with method overrides to ensure variety among individual units. Certain enemies move strategically, forcing the player to focus more intently on aiming to take them down. The game features Particle Systems triggered when taking damage, attacking enemies or when they are defeated, as well as distinct sound effects corresponding to each of the aforementioned features. Paths are implemented for an enemy type that shoots projectiles, so that they strafe around the player instead of charging at the player, creating a slight layer of complexity to them. The actions of enemies occur around Timelines, with a unique timeline occurring for each enemy’s attack intervals.
