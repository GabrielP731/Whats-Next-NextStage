The game's story centers on Orion, who, after interacting with a time machine, travels to the medieval era—specifically, to the outskirts of a kingdom. This kingdom mistakes Orion for an enemy intent on destroying their castle. Consequently, your objective is to play as Orion and survive multiple waves of enemies while the time portal recharges, allowing you to escape back to your own time.

The game is an RPG/Tower Defense hybrid in which you must eliminate all enemies attempting to breach your base. As you progress, your base becomes sturdier and more resilient; simultaneously, you gain power through a separate upgrade system.

How to Play:
Mouse Movement: Aim/Adjust bullet trajectory
Left Click: Fire
E: Purchase an Upgrade Card (if funds are available)

Cheat Keys:
C: Gain 10,000 coins
Ctrl: View stats and multipliers
L: Level up the tower

Objective: Survive Wave 5

Game Theme: Evolution. This theme is demonstrated at Tower Levels 3 and 5, where the tower transforms into a larger, more powerful structure. The card system also represents a form of progression; you begin with minimal damage output and defenses, but—provided you acquire enough upgrade cards—you can eventually become an indestructible force. Furthermore, the number of enemies increases with each wave, and their stats progressively rise for every wave the player successfully completes.

Regarding the "Graduation Project" requirements: The minimap fulfills the "View/Perspective" criteria. The enemies are implemented using inheritance with method overrides to ensure variety among individual units. Certain enemies move strategically, forcing the player to focus more intently on aiming to take them down. The game features special effects triggered when attacking enemies or when they are defeated, as well as distinct sound effects corresponding to each of the aforementioned features.
